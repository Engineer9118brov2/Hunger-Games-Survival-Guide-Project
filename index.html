The Cornucopia is the heart of chaos and entertainment in the first day of the Hunger Games. The Cornucopia contains supplies placed by the Gamemakers that help tributes survive with items decreasing in value the further away they are from the horn. Best Strategies to Survive the Games

Survival isn't just about supplies, it's also about strategy. Based on my many years of experience, I noticed that the tributes with the best tactics and half decent strength have a good chance of winning. Here are some tactics that should be adapted based on the arena's environment.

  1. Daniil

    Assess the Situation at First: If you're strong like a Career run for the Cornucopia for supplies. In other cases, run to the edges of the arena becuase many tributes die in the first minutes due to the strength of the Careers.

  2. Lochlan

    Prioritize Water: Dehydration is one of the main problems in survival. Make sure to locate streams or lakes early in the games so you can find it incase of an emergency (for example you are injured and you need water to sooth the wound). Remember to purify the water with iodine found in the Cornucopia.

  3. Mason

    Get Food: Use skills such as traping and snares to catch animals using the wire coil that was in the supplies. You can also collect herbs and berries as a secondary source: remember eat only the food that you know for sure that is safe.

  4. Daniil

    Form Alliances: Partner with trustable tributes for protection and resources, but be ready to betray them when it gets to the finally. Trust is only temporary.

  5. Lochlan

    Camouflage and Hide: Blend into nature using mud, leaves, or paint. Climb trees for safety and better visuals, like Rue.

  6. Mason

    Avoid the Careers Early: They hunt in groups so don't approach them till they are weakened. Use the size of the arena to your advantage.

  7. Daniil

    Manage Fire Carefully: Great for warmth and cooking, but smoke gives away your position. Build small, controlled fires or use them as traps.

  8. Lochlan

    Play to Sponsors: Show personality during training and interviews for in the Games gifts like medicine. A likable tribute gets all the supplies when needed.

  9. Mason

    Set Traps and Alarms: Use wire or rope to booby-trap areas, alerting you of intruders or food.

  10. Lochlan

    Stay Mobile and Alert: Don't stay in one spot for too long. Move quietly, listen for cannons, and adapt to Gamemaker twists like fires or mutts.
